# $1,000 — An Interactive Social Simulation

An interactive browser game exploring how cash transfers, kinship obligations, and rumours shape child survival and poverty in rural Kenya.

### [🎮 Play Online](https://jinlinghuang42-cmd.github.io/one-thousand-dollars-game/)

**Best played on a desktop browser.** No installation required.

## About the Game

You play as Akinyi, a pregnant woman in rural Kenya who is about to receive a $1,000 cash transfer.

Over four chapters, you make decisions about healthcare, nutrition, family obligations, and investment while trying to keep your child alive until age five.

Your choices affect household resources, social relationships, and child survival. The game features seven possible endings.

## Research Background

The game draws on empirical research in development economics, including cash transfer experiments, informal risk-sharing networks, and poverty traps.

It uses a stylized simulation to explore how social interactions and household decisions may shape the outcomes of financial assistance.

**Note:** Simulation results are model-generated and should not be interpreted as empirical causal estimates.
